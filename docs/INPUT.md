# Input

Every touch, click and key Temperance answers, and what you see happen. Other
documents cite this one.

| Task | Touch | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notifications & Events | Tap the bell | — |
| Control Center | Tap the network icon | — |
| System Tray | Tap the tray icon | — |
| Calendar | Tap the clock | — |
| Weather | Tap the weather icon | — |

## Status Bar

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the icon of the page already open | Closes the page. |
| Tap or click above or below an icon | Counts as that icon. |
| Your finger on an icon | It lifts until you let go. |
| Pointer over the weather, network, tray or battery icon | It grows a little and shows its name. |
| On a thin bar, tap the icons, or pull down from them | A tap opens or closes the nearest icon's page. Pulled, they grow finger-sized below it: slide to one, which is marked and named, and let go to open it, or in the bar or past an end to cancel. |
| Escape, with a page open | Closes the page. |
| Tap or click the pin in a page's header | Keeps the page open when focus leaves; shown only when turned on in settings. |

## Notifications & Events

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the bell | Opens Notifications & Events, which reads what waited, so the ticker clears. |
| Tap or click a notification | Runs its default action, else opens its application. |
| Tap or click an action pill | Runs that action only. |
| Tap or click Dismiss on a notification | Removes that notification. |
| Tap or click Dismiss on an event | Removes it from the ticker and the history until tomorrow. |
| Tap or click Clear beside an application | Removes that application's notifications. |
| Tap or click Clear beside Recent | Removes every notification; today's events stay. |
| Tap or click Expand or Collapse on an application | Opens or folds its group. |
| Tap or click Show more or Show less | Opens or folds a long notification in place. |
| Wheel over the history | Scrolls 80 px a notch at Plasma's usual three lines a notch. |
| Tap or click do not disturb | Turns it on or off. While on, the ticker stays still and the bell checks what arrives. |
| Enter or Space on a focused notification | Same as tapping it. |

### Event ticker

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Pointer resting on the bell | The bell takes the highlight color and the ticker shows its latest line again. |
| Tap or click the ticker's line | Opens Notifications & Events. |
| Flick or drag the ticker's line sideways | Checks it off: a notification waits unread in the history, today's event is marked seen, and the next line comes up. The bell counts lines still waiting, then shows ✓ until the history opens. Let go early and it springs back. |
| Flick sideways over the ticker with no line showing | Brings up the latest line, setting nothing aside; after a touch it stays a few seconds. |

### Important banners

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click a banner | Runs its default action, else opens it in the history. |
| Tap or click Dismiss | Removes the notification. |
| Tap or click Keep for review | Moves the notification to the history. |
| Tap or click Show more | Moves the notification to the history and opens it there. |
| Enter or Space on a focused banner button | Same as tapping it. |

## Control Center

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the network icon | Opens Control Center. |
| Tap or click the battery in the bar | Opens Plasma's Power and Battery page, else Control Center. |
| Drag the volume or brightness slider | Sets the level. |
| Tap or click the sound or brightness icon | Opens that control's full page. |
| Tap or click the profile entry | Opens Performance, to apply or edit profiles. |
| Right-click, press and hold, or Menu key on it | Lists the profiles; choosing one applies it. |
| Tap or click Add new device on the Bluetooth page | Opens KDE's pairing wizard. |
| Tap or click Back | Returns to the page that opened this one. |
| Tap, click, right-click, middle-click, scroll or press and hold a pill | As on that entry in the System Tray. |

### Performance

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a profile under Profile | Applies it at once. |
| Drag the charge limit | Sets where charging stops, 40 to 100% in fives, on release; the profile in use keeps it. |
| Turn the refresh-rate switch on or off | Sets the built-in screen to its fastest or slowest rate; away from the profile's rate it reads Manual until the profile changes. |
| Turn Switch when plugged in or unplugged on or off | Starts or stops changing profile with the power source. |
| Choose the Plugged in or On battery profile | Sets that power source's profile, while switching is on. |
| Tap or click Edit a profile | Opens or folds the editor. |
| Choose the profile to edit | Loads its settings into the editor. |
| New, a name, then Create | Saves the edited profile, with its changes, as a new one. |
| Tap or click a Performance profile or Energy use pill | Stages that choice for the profile being edited. |
| Drag a power-limit slider | Stages that limit, in watts. |
| Tap or click Reset beside Power limits or Fan curve | Stages the profile's own limits, or fans run by the firmware. |
| Drag a point on the fan curve | Moves it, neighbours making way, showing its temperature and fan speed. A touch elsewhere scrolls. |
| Tap or click Save to, or Enter or Space on it | Saves everything staged to that profile; dimmed until something changes. |

### Session actions

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click Sleep | Sleeps at once. |
| Tap or click Lock | Locks the screen at once. |
| Tap or click Restart | Plasma asks first, then restarts. |
| Tap or click Shut down | Plasma asks first, then shuts down. |
| Tap or click More | Opens the other actions; Plasma confirms Log out. |
| Tap or click Switch user | Opens the switch-user screen. |

## System Tray

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the tray icon | Opens the System Tray. |
| Tap or click an entry | Opens its page, or acts as its application says and closes the popup, except Kadunce's Cards switch, which keeps it open. |
| Right-click or press and hold an entry | Opens the entry's own menu. |
| Middle-click an entry | Runs its secondary action, as in Plasma's tray. |
| Wheel over an entry | Passes the scroll to the entry, such as the volume. |
| Enter or Space on a focused entry | Same as tapping it. |
| Menu key on a focused entry | Opens the entry's own menu. |
| Tap or click Open system settings | Opens System Settings. |
| Tap or click Configure system tray icons | Opens Temperance's settings. |

## Calendar

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the clock | Opens or closes the calendar on this month. |
| Tap or click a day | Shows its holidays and events. |
| Swipe sideways across the days | Turns the month; left is next. |
| Wheel or touchpad scroll over the days | A month per notch or gesture; down is later. |
| Tap or click the month arrows | Shows the previous or next month. |
| Tap or click Today | Returns to today; shown away from it. |
| Tap or click Smaller or Larger calendar | The month alone, or with the next days. |

## Weather

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Tap or click the weather icon | Opens the weather page. |
| Tap or click Open weather | Opens the chosen weather application. |

## Settings

| Input | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Right-click Temperance, then Configure Temperance | Opens Tray Entries, Appearance and Calendar, or Shuffle Settings if installed. |
| Paste a private calendar address, then Add | Links that calendar; events appear within minutes. |
| Choose a linked calendar's color | Its events' dots take it. |
| Remove a linked calendar | Unlinks it, forgetting its color. |
| Notifications off in Appearance | Plasma's own notifications show them. |
| Setting "Scroll notifications in the ticker", off | The bell counts what waits; nothing scrolls. |
| Choose a clock font | Sets the clock's typeface from a list. |
